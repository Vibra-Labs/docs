Atrium distinguishes between two kinds of people in your organization: **team members** (your staff) and **clients** (your customers). Both are managed from the **People** section in the dashboard, which has separate **Team** and **Clients** tabs.

This page covers team members — the admins and owners who have access to the dashboard. For inviting clients, see the Clients tab on the same page.

---

## Roles overview

Atrium uses three roles across the platform:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Owner">
    Full access to everything in the organization. Owners can invite and remove team members, change roles, delete projects, and access all settings. There is no restriction on how many owners an organization can have. Only owners can invite other team members.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Admin">
    Can create, edit, and archive projects, manage clients, create and send invoices, upload files, and post updates. Admins cannot invite or remove other team members, change roles, or delete projects.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Member (client)">
    Portal-only access. Members see only the projects they are assigned to. They cannot access the dashboard at all. This role is assigned automatically when you invite a client.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  The **Member** role is for clients only. When you invite a team member, you choose between **Admin** and **Owner**.
</Note>

## Inviting a team member

Only **owners** can invite new team members.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to People">
    In the dashboard sidebar, click **People**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Team tab">
    Click the **Team** tab at the top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the invite form">
    Enter the team member's email address and select their role (**Admin** or **Owner**) from the dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Click **Invite**. Atrium sends an invitation email and generates a unique invite link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the invite link">
    After the invitation is created, Atrium displays the invite link on screen. Copy it and share it directly if the email doesn't reach them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The invited person clicks the link, creates or signs in to their account, and is added to your organization automatically.

<Tip>
  Pending invitations appear in the **Team** tab with a **Copy Link** button so you can re-share the link at any time before it is accepted.
</Tip>

## Changing a team member's role

Owners can change the role of any team member except themselves.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the team member">
    On the **Team** tab of the People page, locate the member in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a new role">
    Click the role badge next to the member's name. A dropdown lets you switch between **Admin** and **Owner**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The role updates immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Removing a team member

Owners can remove any team member except themselves.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the team member">
    On the **Team** tab of the People page, locate the member in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the remove icon">
    Click the trash icon next to the member's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm the removal in the dialog. The member loses access to the dashboard and all projects immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Removing a team member does not delete any content they created (projects, updates, tasks, files, or invoices). Their work remains intact.
</Warning>

## Team members vs. clients

The key distinction is dashboard access:

| | Team member (admin/owner) | Client (member) |
|---|---|---|
| Dashboard access | Yes | No |
| Portal access | Yes | Yes |
| Create projects | Yes (admin/owner) | No |
| View all projects | Yes | Assigned only |
| Create invoices | Yes (admin/owner) | No |
| Pay invoices | No | Yes (via portal) |
| Post updates | Yes | Yes (assigned projects) |
| Invite others | Owner only | No |

Clients are invited separately from the **Clients** tab on the People page, not from the Team tab. The invitation flow is the same, but the resulting role is always **Member**.
