---
title: "White-Label Branding"
description: "Customize your client portal with your own logo, colors, and company name."
---

Atrium's client portal is fully white-labeled. Your clients see your logo, your colors, and your company name — not Atrium's. All branding settings live in **Settings → System** under the **Branding** section.

<Note>
  Only owners and admins can update branding settings.
</Note>

## What branding applies to

Your branding settings affect:

- The client portal at `<your-atrium-domain>/portal` — logo in the header and sidebar, primary and accent colors throughout the UI
- The dashboard sidebar — your logo and company name appear there for your team as well

## Updating your branding

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open System Settings">
    In the dashboard, navigate to **Settings → System**. Scroll to the **Branding** section.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your company name">
    Enter your company name in the **Company Name** field. This appears in the portal header and the dashboard sidebar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload your logo">
    Click the upload area (or drag and drop a file onto it) to upload your logo.

    - Supported formats: **PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP**
    - Maximum size: **5 MB**
    - The logo is displayed in the portal header and the dashboard sidebar.

    Once a logo is uploaded, use the **Replace** button to swap it or **Remove** to delete it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your colors">
    Set a **Primary Color** and an **Accent Color** using the color pickers or by entering a hex value (e.g. `#006b68`).

    - **Primary Color** — used for buttons, active states, and key UI elements
    - **Accent Color** — used for secondary highlights and decorative elements

    A live preview below the color pickers shows how your logo and colors will look together.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Optionally hide the logo">
    Check **Hide logo in sidebar** if you don't have a logo ready yet or prefer a text-only header. This hides the logo in both the dashboard sidebar and the portal header, falling back to your company name.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your settings">
    Click **Save Settings** at the bottom of the form. Your branding is applied immediately — refresh the portal to see the changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Portal URL

Your clients access the branded portal at:

```
https://<your-atrium-domain>/portal
```

The `/portal` path is fixed. To serve the portal at a custom domain or subdomain (e.g. `portal.agency.com`), see [Custom Domain](/branding/custom-domain).

## Color format

Both color fields accept standard 6-digit hex values. Enter the `#` prefix:

```
#006b68
```

Values outside this format are rejected by Atrium when saving.

<Tip>
  Use your brand's primary button color for the **Primary Color** field. Clients will see it on the **Pay Invoice** button and other call-to-action elements throughout the portal.
</Tip>
