## Get up and running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deploy Atrium">
    Pull the Docker image and start the container with a single command. See the [Quick Start](/quickstart).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Open your Atrium URL and sign up. The first account becomes the organization owner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your first client">
    Create a project, then invite your client by email. They get access to the branded portal at `/portal`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize your portal">
    Add your logo and brand colors so the portal looks like yours. See [White-Label Branding](/branding/white-label).
  </Step>
</Steps>
