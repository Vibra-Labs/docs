The client portal is a dedicated interface for your clients — separate from your team's dashboard. It lives at `/portal` on your Atrium URL and gives each client a focused view of their own work. Your branding (logo and colors) is applied automatically so the portal feels like a native part of your agency's product.

<Note>
  Clients only see projects, files, and invoices that you have explicitly assigned to them. They have no visibility into other clients' work or your internal dashboard.
</Note>

---

## What clients see

When a client logs in to the portal they land on their **Projects** list. From there, every project detail page has four tabs:

| Tab | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Updates** | Progress notes posted by you or the client, with image and file attachments |
| **Tasks** | Checklist tasks and decision polls created by your team |
| **Files** | Files uploaded to the project, with download links |
| **Invoices** | Invoices issued against the project, with payment links |

The project sidebar shows the project name, description, a read-only status progress bar, and the project timeline (start and end dates) when set.

---

## Client authentication

Clients can sign in using either method:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Magic link">
    The client enters their email address on the login page. Atrium sends a one-time sign-in link to their inbox. Clicking the link logs them in immediately — no password required.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Email and password">
    Clients who prefer a password can set one from their account settings in the portal. They sign in with their email address and password on the standard login screen.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Clients accept their initial invitation via a unique link you generate from the **People → Clients** section of the dashboard. See [Team Members](/features/team) for how to invite clients.

## Notifications

Atrium keeps clients informed of activity on their projects through two notification channels:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="In-app notifications">
    A notification indicator appears in the portal when there are new updates, file uploads, or invoices on the client's projects. Notifications are fetched by real-time polling so the count updates without requiring a page refresh.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Push notifications">
    Clients can opt in to web push notifications (Web Push / VAPID). Once enabled, they receive browser notifications for new activity even when the portal tab is not open. Clients can manage this preference from their portal settings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Paying invoices

Clients can pay invoices directly from the portal without leaving your branded experience. On the **Invoices** tab of any project, an unpaid invoice shows a **Pay** button that opens a Stripe Checkout session. After a successful payment, the invoice status updates to **Paid** in both the portal and your dashboard.

<Note>
  Invoice payments require Stripe to be configured on your Atrium instance. See the [Payments Overview](/payments/overview) for instructions.
</Note>

## Portal branding

The portal inherits your organization's branding settings automatically. You can customize:

- **Logo** — Uploaded from **Settings → Branding**. The logo appears in the portal header and is also used as the browser favicon.
- **Primary color** — Sets the accent color used for buttons, links, and the active status indicator in the project progress bar.

Changes take effect immediately for all clients.

---

## Client account settings

Clients can manage their own account from the **Settings** page in the portal (`/portal/settings`). They can:

- Update their display name
- Change their password
- Update their profile (company, phone, address, website, description)
- Delete their account

Profile data entered by clients is also visible to you in the **People → Clients** section of the dashboard.

---

## Portal navigation

The portal sidebar links to the following sections:

- **Projects** — All projects assigned to the client
- **Settings** — Account and profile settings

There is no separate files or invoices listing at the top level — clients access files and invoices through the project they belong to.
